title: Wild Yeast

---

Saccharomyces cerevisiae is the domesticated workhorse of both brewing and
baking, selected over centuries for predictable attenuation and clean
flavour. Wild yeasts are its undomesticated relatives, present on grain, on
fruit skins and in the air of any room where fermentation happens regularly.

An uninoculated ferment is unpredictable because the outcome depends on
whichever organisms happen to arrive first and tolerate the conditions best.
Brettanomyces, a common wild genus, ferments slowly and produces distinctly
funky, barnyard-like aromatics that are a prized feature in some styles and
a contamination in others.
