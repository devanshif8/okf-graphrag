---
title: Brewing Water Chemistry
description: Sulfate and chloride shift perceived bitterness and body; the ratio between them shapes the beer's balance.
status: published
---

Two ions do most of the audible work in brewing water. Sulfate accentuates
bitterness and makes a finish read as drier and more mineral. Chloride does
close to the opposite, rounding the palate and making the beer taste fuller
and sweeter. Neither is a flavour on its own; both change how other flavours
land.

Brewers therefore talk about the sulfate-to-chloride ratio rather than
absolute amounts. A ratio well above one suits a bitter, hop-forward beer. A
ratio below one suits a malt-forward one. Roughly equal is the neutral
starting point when you do not yet know what the beer wants.
